Where to set up a table

  1. Now
    Heart of the City Farmers Market

    Free tables for nonprofits offering something to the community, set up wherever there's open space. Ask at the market info tent at Larkin and Fulton on a Wednesday or Sunday.

    Market's tabling page
  2. Now
    San Francisco Public Library

    Offer a free program, like a short breathing or meditation session, through the library's program proposal form. Branches also host free community events in their meeting rooms.

    Present a program
  3. Now
    City College, Ocean campus

    Sign in at the Student Activities Office (Student Union, room 205) before handing out flyers. Ram Plaza and the Cloud Science Mall are allowed spots, and the same office posts flyers on campus boards. Ask them about a table.

    Campus guidelines
  4. Other markets
    Ferry Plaza, Alemany and the rest

    None of them post a table program for community groups. Ask at each market's info booth, since space and rules vary week to week.

  5. Next year
    Sunday Streets

    A 10 by 10 spot on a car-free street. Free for groups based in that neighborhood, otherwise $45 to $65 for small nonprofits, and the fee can be waived. You must offer a free activity, and no fundraising. The 2026 deadlines have passed, so watch for the 2027 form.
