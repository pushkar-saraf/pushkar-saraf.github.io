Campus tables and posting

Who lets outside groups table or post, as of November 2026. Rules change by term, so check before you go.

Tables and approvals

  1. Now
    City College, Ram Plaza

    Sign in at the Student Activities Office, Student Union 205, then hand out or post on the boards.

  2. Now
    UC Berkeley, Sproul Plaza

    Anyone can hand out non-commercial flyers, 6 am to midnight. No table without a student group.

  3. Now
    Laney College

    Outside groups can submit flyers for approval in 24 to 48 hours.

  4. Now
    Stanford White Plaza

    Post on the public kiosks and hand out leaflets. No tables for visitors.

  5. Ask
    SKY club at Berkeley

    A student club could sponsor a Sproul table. Its current registration isn't confirmed.

    Details
  6. Next term
    SF State lobby tables

    Outside groups can rent a table with an EIN letter and insurance.

  7. Ask
    UCSF

    Only on invitation from a UCSF campus group.

  8. Ask
    SJSU, USF and Cal State East Bay

    Contact student life first. SJSU books tables only for campus groups, and USF doesn't post outside groups' flyers.

A volunteer guide. On campus, outside groups usually may hand out non-commercial flyers outdoors but post only on approved boards, and never inside dorms. A paid course flyer may count as commercial, so a flyer for a free intro session is safest. Checked November 2026.
